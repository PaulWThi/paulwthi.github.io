Privacy policy — Aiya Walk Sync

Effective October 6, 2026. This policy describes the personal-use Aiya Walk Sync tool and this public information website.

Google data accessed and used

The tool accesses the contents and metadata of the Google Drive files designated by the account owner for context synchronization. It uploads the owner-selected document and reads the resulting copies to verify their contents. The configured Google Drive authorization can technically permit broader access; the sync workflow operates on its designated targets.

Storage and retention

The local source document remains on the owner's computer. Synced copies remain in the owner's Google Drive, subject to the owner's sharing, deletion, trash and version-history settings. OAuth credentials are stored privately on the owner's computer and are not included in the public website or source repository. Temporary download copies used for verification are removed by the sync workflow.

Sharing and assistant access

The sync tool does not publish the document on this website, sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train a generalized AI model. Google processes files to provide Drive. At the owner's direction, a connected assistant such as ChatGPT may retrieve the context document; that assistant's data handling is governed by its provider and the owner's account settings. Do not include information you do not want that assistant to receive.

Control, removal and revocation

The owner can stop synchronization, delete local and Drive copies, change file sharing, and revoke the application's access through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops API use but does not automatically delete files already stored in Drive or copies previously shared with an assistant.

Limited use

Aiya Walk Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

This site contains public information only. It does not collect Google login credentials or embed analytics, tracking scripts, or advertising. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and changes

Contact the application operator using the support email shown on Google's authorization screen. This policy will be updated if the tool's data practices change.
