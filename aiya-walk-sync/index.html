Aiya Walk Sync

A personal tool that keeps an owner-selected context document in Google Drive up to date for ongoing planning and assistant conversations.

What it does

The owner maintains a local context document. When the owner requests a sync, Aiya Walk Sync updates the owner's designated Markdown and plain-text copies in Google Drive, then downloads both copies to verify that their contents match.

The tool uses Google account authorization to access those files. It is for personal use, has no public signup, and does not make the owner's documents public. Access to a synced document follows its Google Drive sharing settings.

Owner-controlled sync

The owner chooses what goes into the document and when it is shared with an assistant. A verified upload is separate from whether a connected assistant has retrieved or indexed the latest version.

For support, contact the operator using the support email shown on the Google authorization screen.
